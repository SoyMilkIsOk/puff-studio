Mobile Bluetooth Guide

Connecting to your Peak Pro or Proxy directly from your phone

🍏 iPhone / iPad (iOS):

Apple blocks Web Bluetooth in Mobile Safari and standard Chrome for iOS. To connect directly via Bluetooth:

  1. Download Path Browser (Puff's official iOS utility) or Bluefy from the App Store.
  2. Open this website inside Path Browser or Bluefy.
  3. Tap Connect and pick your Puff from the Bluetooth prompt!

🤖 Android & Desktop:

Web Bluetooth is supported natively in Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge, and Samsung Internet. Simply tap Connect.

⚠️ Can't Find Your Device?

  • Close the official Puff App: Puff devices only support one Bluetooth connection at a time. If your phone or another app is connected, the Puff stops broadcasting!
  • Pairing Mode: Hold the Puff power button for 6 seconds until the lights glow pulsing blue.
  • Renamed Device: Tap Scan All in the top header to show all nearby Bluetooth devices without name filters.
  • Desktop Permissions: On macOS, ensure Chrome has Bluetooth access enabled in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Bluetooth.

💡 Need Pairing Help?

Tap the ℹ️ Information icon next to the Connect button at the top of the page for an interactive 3-step visual pairing guide.
